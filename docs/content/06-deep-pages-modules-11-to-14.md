# 11. Library Management

**H1:** A modern library, digitised and organised.

## The purpose
Library Management lets a school run its library without registers: students find and reserve books, the librarian issues and returns them by scanning, and fines and overdue books look after themselves.

## The problem it replaces
Issue registers make lending slow. Books are hard to find or reserve. Overdue books and fines are tracked by hand, so they are missed.

## How to use it

**Set up once**
1. **Build the catalogue.** Add each title and copy, and put a barcode on every book.
2. **Set the lending rules** and the fine rate.

**Every day**
1. **Issue and return by scanning** the book and the student.
2. **Students search and reserve** from the app, so the book is waiting for them.
3. **Overdue books and fines** are tracked automatically, with a notice to the student.

**Every term**
- Review **reading progress** and the most borrowed titles.
- Reconcile the **book stock** in the inventory.

## Example
_Example: a student searches the catalogue from her phone, finds the title is out, reserves it, and is told when it returns. The librarian scans it back in and the reservation is ready._

## How each person uses it
- **Librarians** issue, return and manage fines. **[confirm: role names]**
- **Students** search and reserve.

## What you get out of it
Faster lending, books students can find, no lost overdue tracking and a record of reading progress.

## How it connects
Barcode scanners, the Parent & Student App, and Academic Inventory Tracking.

## Questions
- *Can students reserve from their phone?* Yes.
- *How are fines handled?* Automatically, with clear records for each student.
- *Do we need special equipment?* Barcode scanning is used for issue and return.

# 12. Transport Management

**H1:** Every bus visible. Every parent informed.

## The purpose
Transport Management lets a school run its buses with confidence: every bus visible, every route planned, every driver and vehicle on record, and every parent told when their child's bus is near.

## The problem it replaces
Parents phone the school to ask where the bus is. Routes are planned on paper. Driver and vehicle details are scattered, and maintenance is remembered, not scheduled.

## How to use it

**Set up once**
1. **Record vehicles, drivers and attendants.**
2. **Plan the routes and stops,** improving them from real data.
3. **Assign students to routes and stops.**
4. **Connect the GPS devices.** **[confirm supported hardware]**

**Every day**
1. **Watch the fleet live** on one screen.
2. **Parents follow their child's bus** in the app, and receive pickup and drop notifications.
3. **Handle exceptions:** a late bus, a changed route.

**Every month**
- Track **fleet maintenance** so vehicles are serviced on time.
- Review routes and **transport fees** in Fee Management.

## Example
_Example: Bus 3 is two stops away. The parents at the next stop get a notification, step out as it arrives, and the school receives no phone calls._

## How each person uses it
- **The transport incharge** plans routes and watches the fleet.
- **Parents** track the bus and get alerts.
- **Principals** know that every child is accounted for.

## What you get out of it
A live view of every vehicle, calmer parents, better routes and vehicles that are maintained on time.

## How it connects
GPS fleet tracking, the Parent & Student App, WhatsApp and SMS alerts, and Fee Management.

## Questions
- *Can parents see the bus?* Yes, live, in the app.
- *Are parents alerted?* Yes, at pickup and drop.
- *Can we manage drivers and vehicles here?* Yes.

# 13. Inspace Performance Insights

**H1:** See how your whole school is performing.

## The purpose
Performance Insights gives leaders one view across academics, attendance, fee collection and staff, so they can compare, spot trouble early and decide from facts.

## The problem it replaces
Standard reports show one area at a time. Comparing sections or years takes manual work. Struggling students are noticed late, after the damage is done.

## How to use it

**Set up once**
1. **Choose the dashboards** you want for each leader.
2. **Define your own KPIs,** the numbers that matter to your school.

**Every week**
1. **Open the school-wide view** and read the headline figures.
2. **Compare cohorts:** sections, subjects and years side by side.
3. **Check the health scores** for attendance and fee collection.
4. **Read the early warnings** and decide who needs help.

**Every term**
- **Benchmark staff** performance.
- **Export insights** for the management review.

## Example
_Example: Class 7 maths results fall two terms in a row while attendance dips. An early warning flags the section; the coordinator speaks to the teacher and the families before the exams._

## How each person uses it
- **School owners and principals** review the whole school.
- **Coordinators** compare sections and subjects.

## What you get out of it
A decision-ready view of the whole school, trends by class and subject, early warnings for students at risk, and exports for meetings.

## How it connects
Academic & Examination, Attendance, Fee Management, Staff Management and Reports & Analytics.

## Questions
- *How is this different from the standard reports?* It combines the areas and compares them.
- *Can we track our own measures?* Yes, with custom KPIs.
- *Can we take it to a meeting?* Yes, insights export for management reviews.

# 14. AI Secretary

**H1:** Ask your school a question. Get the answer.

_The full marketing page for AI Secretary is in `03-ai-secretary-full-page.md`. This is the user's guide to it._

## The purpose
AI Secretary lets the people who run the school ask for what they need in plain language, and get it from the school's own data, without opening a report or waiting for someone to prepare one.

## The problem it replaces
One answer means opening several reports. Notices take time to draft. Parents ask the same routine questions, again and again.

## How to use it

**Getting started**
1. **Open the management app** and go to AI Secretary.
2. **Ask a question** in your own words, about attendance, fees, admissions and more.
3. **Read the answer,** which comes from your school's data.

**Every day**
1. **Read the briefing** waiting for you each morning, daily or weekly as you choose.
2. **Act on an alert** when something unusual happens, such as an attendance drop.
3. **Draft a notice.** Ask it to write a notice or circular, edit it, and send it through Communication Management.

**For parents**
- Routine parent questions are answered automatically, so the front office is free for the ones that need a person.

## Good habits for asking
- Be specific about the date, class or area ("today", "this month", "Class 7").
- Ask one thing at a time; then ask the next.
- Treat a draft notice as a first draft and review it before it goes out.

## Example
_Example: a principal types, "How many students were absent today?" and sees a count. She then asks about fee collection for the month, and then asks it to draft a notice about a holiday._

## How each person uses it
- **Principals and school owners** ask questions and read briefings.
- **Administrators** draft notices and circulars.

## What you get out of it
Answers in seconds, briefings without effort, early warning of changes and notices drafted for you.

## How it connects
Attendance, Fee Management, Admissions, Communication Management (notices go out through it), and Performance Insights.

## Questions
- *What can I ask?* Attendance, fees, admissions and more, in plain language.
- *Does it use only my school's data?* Yes, your own data. **[confirm: how it respects each user's role permissions]**
- *Can it write notices?* Yes, in seconds.
- *Where do I use it?* In the management app.
