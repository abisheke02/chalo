# 1. Admissions Management

**H1:** Admissions that run themselves, from first enquiry to first day.

## The purpose
Admissions Management exists so that no family who shows interest in your school is lost, and so that every step from a first enquiry to a child's first day happens in one place. It turns a pile of forms, phone calls and spreadsheets into a single pipeline you can see.

## The problem it replaces
Enquiries are written in a register or a spreadsheet. Application forms are paper. Documents arrive in pieces and are chased by phone. Nobody knows how many enquiries became admissions, so nobody knows why the others did not.

## How to use it

**Set up once, before the admission season**
1. **Build the application form.** Choose the fields your school needs, including custom ones, so parents fill in exactly what you ask for.
2. **Decide the documents you require,** so parents know what to upload.
3. **Set the messages parents receive** as an application moves along, by SMS and WhatsApp.
4. **Set your sibling and waitlist rules,** so brothers and sisters and a full class are handled the same way every time.

**For every enquiry**
1. **Capture it.** The parent applies online, or the front office enters the enquiry.
2. **The parent is kept informed automatically.** Each change in status sends a message, so nobody phones to ask.
3. **Documents are uploaded and verified.** The administrator checks each one in a verification step.
4. **The principal approves.** If the class is full, the child joins the waitlist.
5. **Fees are paid online** where the school collects an application or admission fee.
6. **The child is enrolled.** The record moves into Student Management with nothing re-typed.

**Each new academic year**
- Use **bulk admission processing** to handle the whole intake at once.
- Open the **conversion view** and read where applicants drop off: enquiry, application, documents, enrolment.

## Example
_A parent enquires on a Monday evening through the online form. By morning she has had a message confirming the enquiry. She uploads the birth certificate from her phone. The administrator verifies it the same day and the principal approves on Wednesday. The family hears at every step and never calls the office._

## How each person uses it
- **Administrators** take enquiries, verify documents and keep the pipeline moving.
- **Principals** approve admissions quickly instead of signing paper.
- **School owners** watch conversion and see admissions beside fees and academics.

## What you get out of it
Every enquiry on record; documents stored and verified; parents updated without effort; and a conversion view that shows where you lose families, so you can fix that stage.

## How it connects
Student Management (the enrolled child's record), Communication (the SMS and WhatsApp messages), Fee Management (online fees).

## Questions
- *Can a parent apply from a phone?* Yes, the form is online.
- *What if we have more applicants than seats?* Waitlist management holds them in order.
- *Can siblings be linked at admission?* Yes.
- *Do we have to re-type the student after admission?* No.

# 2. Student Management

**H1:** Every student's full record, one click away.

## The purpose
Student Management is the school's single source of truth about each child. Whoever needs to know something about a student, whether a teacher, the front office or a parent, finds the same answer in the same place.

## The problem it replaces
Records live in cupboards of files and in several spreadsheets. A mark sheet is in one place, a transfer certificate in another, a parent's phone number in a third. Finding anything takes time, and promoting a school to the next year takes days.

## How to use it

**Set up once**
1. **Decide what a profile holds:** details, a photo ID, emergency contacts and medical notes.
2. **Bring existing records in.** New students arrive from Admissions; current students are loaded at onboarding, and data migration is part of every plan.

**Every day**
1. **Find a student instantly** by searching, and see everything about them on one profile.
2. **Keep it current:** update contacts, add medical notes, upload documents such as birth and transfer certificates.
3. **Link siblings,** so a family is one connected group across the school.
4. **Let history build itself.** Attendance, marks and fees accumulate on the profile as the year goes on.

**Every year**
- Use **bulk promotion** to move whole classes to the next academic year in one step.
- When a child leaves, their record and documents stay complete. For CBSE and State board schools the platform supports transfer certificate generation.

## Example
_A parent calls to ask for a copy of last year's mark sheet. The front office searches the child's name, opens the profile and has the academic history on screen before the call ends._

## How each person uses it
- **Administrators** keep records accurate and documents filed.
- **Teachers** see the students they teach.
- **Parents** see their child's information in the app.

## What you get out of it
A complete, searchable record of every child from admission to alumni, with no files to dig through, and a year-end promotion that takes minutes.

## How it connects
Admissions (where the record starts), Attendance, Fee Management, Academic & Examination (results feed the history), and the Parent & Student App.

## Questions
- *Can we store documents?* Yes, such as birth and transfer certificates.
- *How do we move to the next class?* Bulk promotion.
- *Are medical notes kept?* Yes, with emergency contacts.
- *Who can see a student's details?* Only the roles you permit.

# 3. Staff Management

**H1:** Your whole team, in one place.

## The purpose
Staff Management keeps everything about the people who run the school in one system: who they are, what they may do, whether they are present and how they are performing.

## The problem it replaces
Staff files are in a cabinet, leave is in a register, appraisals are on paper and nobody has the whole picture. Leave requests wait for a signature, and a teacher's absence is discovered at the last minute.

## How to use it

**Set up once**
1. **Create staff records** and attach their documents.
2. **Set roles and access** so each person sees only what their job needs.
3. **Set up the leave approval route,** so each request goes to the right person.

**Every day**
1. **Staff attendance and leave are recorded** in the system.
2. **A staff member requests leave;** the approver sees it and approves or declines.
3. **Use the directory** to find any colleague's contact details.

**Every term or year**
- Run **appraisals** and track performance in the same place.
- Check **leave and attendance patterns** before payroll.

## Example
_A teacher applies for two days of leave. The principal approves from her phone. The timetable can then assign a substitute and payroll already knows._

## How each person uses it
- **Principals** see staff performance and leave at a glance and approve requests.
- **School owners** see the team beside the rest of the school.
- **Administrators** keep records and documents complete.

## What you get out of it
Complete staff records, fast leave approvals, a clear performance record and accurate inputs for payroll.

## How it connects
Payroll (salary follows attendance and leave), Attendance, and AI Timetable (absence triggers a substitution).

## Questions
- *Can leave be approved online?* Yes.
- *Can different staff see different things?* Yes, through role-based access.
- *Can we run appraisals?* Yes, with appraisal and performance tracking.

# 4. Fee Management

**H1:** Collect fees on time, without chasing a single parent.

## The purpose
Fee Management makes sure the school collects what it is owed, on time, with a clear record of every rupee. It also gives parents an easy way to pay, and gives management a live view of collection against target.

## The problem it replaces
Fee follow-ups are done by phone and by hand. Receipts are matched against bank entries in a spreadsheet. Defaulters are discovered late. The principal asks "how much have we collected?" and the answer takes a day.

## How to use it

**Set up once, before the year**
1. **Build the fee plans:** multi-instalment plans with your own due dates, for each class or category.
2. **Add the other fees** that sit beside tuition: transport, hostel and activity fees.
3. **Set concessions and scholarships,** so they are applied correctly to the right students.
4. **Connect the payment gateway,** so parents can pay online. Connect Tally if your accountant uses it.

**Every day**
1. **Parents pay online** at any time through the gateway, or at the counter. Both are recorded in one ledger.
2. **A receipt is issued** straight away, GST-ready.
3. **Reminders go out automatically** by SMS and WhatsApp for dues that are approaching or overdue, until they are cleared.
4. **The accountant reconciles** daily. Payments are matched to the right student, and duplicates are prevented.

**Every month**
- Open the **collection dashboard** and compare against the target.
- Review the **defaulter list** with the principal.
- Pull **reports** for management and audit.

## Example
_Example figures: a school sets a monthly target of ₹17.4 lakh. By the 20th the dashboard shows ₹18.4 lakh collected, 5.7 per cent over target. Fourteen defaulters have already had reminders; the accountant sees the list without asking anyone._

## How each person uses it
- **Accountants** set up plans, reconcile, issue receipts and report.
- **Parents** pay without queues and see their dues in the app.
- **School owners** and **principals** watch collection against target.

## What you get out of it
Faster collection, no manual follow-ups, a ledger where every receipt is accounted for, and audit-ready reports.

## How it connects
Tally ERP, the online payment gateway, WhatsApp and SMS (reminders), Transport (transport fees), Payroll, and the Parent & Student App.

## Questions
- *Can parents pay by themselves?* Yes, online at any time.
- *Do reminders go out by themselves?* Yes, until dues are cleared.
- *Can we apply concessions and scholarships?* Yes.
- *Does it work with Tally?* Yes. **[confirm one-way or two-way]**

# 5. AI Powered Timetable & Scheduling

**H1:** A conflict-free timetable, built for you.

## The purpose
The timetable module produces a school timetable that works: no teacher in two places, no room double-booked, and a fair load on every teacher. Then it keeps the day running when someone is absent.

## The problem it replaces
A timetable built by hand takes days of juggling in a spreadsheet. Clashes are found after printing. Every absence means a scramble to find cover.

## How to use it

**Set up once per term**
1. **Enter the facts:** teachers, subjects, classes and sections, rooms, and shifts if you run more than one.
2. **State the constraints:** teacher availability and preferences, room limits, and how many periods each subject needs.
3. **Choose how to balance workload** across the week.

**Generate**
1. **Let the AI build the timetable.** It works around every constraint, with no clashes.
2. **Review and adjust.** Fine-tune what you wish.
3. **Publish and export** to PDF, print, or digital display boards.

**Every day**
1. **A teacher is absent.** The system assigns a substitute instantly.
2. **The substitute is alerted.** The class is covered without a scramble.

## Example
_A coordinator enters 40 teachers, 24 sections and the period needs for each subject. The AI returns a timetable with no clashes. The coordinator moves two periods by preference and prints it._

## How each person uses it
- **Coordinators** generate, adjust and publish.
- **Teachers** receive their schedule and substitution alerts.
- **Principals** see the school day run without disruption.

## What you get out of it
A clash-free timetable in a fraction of the time, balanced teacher loads, and instant cover for absences.

## How it connects
Staff Management (leave and absence), Attendance, and Academic & Examination (exam scheduling).

## Questions
- *Can it handle two shifts?* Yes, multi-shift and multi-section are supported.
- *Can teachers state preferences?* Yes.
- *What if the AI's version isn't right?* You can adjust it.
- *Can we display it on screens?* Yes, on digital display boards.
