## Section 2 · The answer, in two sentences (for Google and AI assistants)

AI Secretary is an assistant built exclusively for school management. It answers questions about attendance, fees and admissions in plain language, sends daily and weekly briefings, flags unusual changes, drafts notices and circulars, and answers routine parent questions.

## Section 3 · The problem

Getting one answer from a school's data usually means opening several reports. Drafting a notice takes time away from teaching. And parents ask the same questions over and over. A principal's day fills with small look-ups.

## Section 4 · Ask it anything (examples, labelled "Sample")

| You ask | You get |
|---------|---------|
| How many students were absent today? | The count, from today's attendance |
| How much fee was collected this month? | The total against the target, from fee records |
| How many admission forms are waiting? | The number and where they stand |

Ask in your own words. AI Secretary answers from your school's data, not from the internet.

## Section 5 · Three things it does without being asked

1. **A briefing at your desk.** A daily or weekly summary of the school is waiting for you.
2. **A tap on the shoulder.** Smart alerts flag unusual changes, such as a drop in attendance, before they become a problem.
3. **A first draft.** It drafts notices and circulars in seconds, ready for you to review and send through Communication Management.

## Section 6 · It also answers your parents

Routine parent questions, the ones the front office answers ten times a day, are answered automatically. Staff get their time back, and parents get an answer at any hour.
