# 6. Attendance Management

**H1:** Attendance in one tap. Parents told in seconds.

## The purpose
Attendance Management records who is in school, tells the right people at once when someone is not, and builds the attendance picture the principal and payroll need.

## The problem it replaces
Paper registers are slow and error-prone. Parents find out about an absence hours later, if at all. Staff attendance and leave sit in separate books, so payroll waits.

## How to use it

**Set up once**
1. **Set up classes and sections** with their student lists.
2. **Connect your biometric or RFID devices** if you use them. **[confirm supported brands]**
3. **Set the absence message** parents will receive.
4. **Set the staff leave approval route.**

**Every day**
1. **Mark attendance.** A teacher marks a whole class in one tap in the app. Where devices are connected, check-ins record attendance automatically.
2. **The parent is told.** The moment a child is marked absent, an SMS or WhatsApp message goes to the parent.
3. **Staff attendance is recorded** the same way, and leave is requested and approved in the system.

**Every week and month**
- Read the **attendance analytics** and trend reports by class.
- Look at who is regularly absent and talk to the families.
- Pass staff attendance to payroll.

## Example
_Example: Class 6B has 40 students. The teacher marks it in one tap by 8:45; two students are absent and two parents receive a message at once. By 9:00 the principal sees school-wide attendance at 96.8 per cent._

## How each person uses it
- **Teachers** mark and review in the app.
- **Principals** see attendance in real time, school-wide.
- **Parents** are alerted when a child is absent.

## What you get out of it
Accurate daily attendance, instant absence alerts, trend reports, and staff attendance that payroll can trust.

## How it connects
Biometric and RFID devices, WhatsApp and SMS, Payroll (attendance-linked deductions), and the Parent & Student App.

## Questions
- *Can we use our current biometric machines?* Yes, biometric and RFID devices are supported.
- *How fast do parents find out?* Instantly after the child is marked absent.
- *Can we see patterns?* Yes, through attendance analytics.

# 7. Academic & Examination

**H1:** From exam schedule to report card, with no spreadsheets.

## The purpose
Academic & Examination runs the whole exam cycle and turns marks into results and report cards the way your board expects, then shows how students, subjects and classes are performing.

## The problem it replaces
Marks are collected on paper, totals and grades are calculated by hand, report cards take days to produce, and an error found at the end means starting again.

## How to use it

**Set up once**
1. **Choose the grading format** for your board: CBSE, ICSE or State.
2. **Create the exam structure:** terms, unit tests and the subjects each covers.

**For every exam**
1. **Schedule it** with flexible exam scheduling.
2. **Teachers enter marks digitally.** Grades are calculated automatically from the marks.
3. **Review the results** for each class.
4. **Generate report cards.** They are produced automatically in the board's format.
5. **Publish results online.** Parents see them in the app as soon as they are published.

**After the exam**
- Open **performance analytics** by student, subject and class.
- Compare terms and years to see the trend.

## Example
_Example: after a unit test, each teacher enters marks from their phone. By the next morning the coordinator has every class's results and the report cards are ready to publish._

## How each person uses it
- **Teachers** enter marks and see grades appear.
- **Coordinators** schedule exams and manage the academic calendar.
- **Parents** view marks and progress in real time.

## What you get out of it
No manual calculation, board-format report cards generated automatically, results published online, and analytics that show where students need help.

## How it connects
Student Management (history), the Parent & Student App, Reports & Analytics, and Performance Insights.

## Questions
- *Which boards are supported?* CBSE, ICSE and State board formats.
- *Are report cards automatic?* Yes, from the marks entered.
- *When do parents see results?* As soon as you publish them.

# 8. Communication Management

**H1:** Every parent, every notice, on time.

## The purpose
Communication Management gives the school one official way to reach parents, so a message goes to exactly the right people on the channel they use, and the school can see who has received and read it.

## The problem it replaces
Updates are scattered across dozens of WhatsApp groups. Messages are missed, replies are lost, and nobody knows who has seen what.

## How to use it

**Set up once**
1. **Connect the channels:** WhatsApp Business API, SMS and voice broadcast.
2. **Set the audiences:** a class, several classes, or the whole school.
3. **Switch on the automatic messages** you want: fee reminders, event reminders, absence alerts.

**Every day**
1. **Write or paste a notice.** AI Secretary can draft notices and circulars for you.
2. **Choose the audience and the channel,** and send.
3. **Watch delivery and reads.** See who received and who opened the message.
4. **Reply in conversation.** Parents and teachers message each other directly.

**For emergencies**
- Send an alert by WhatsApp, SMS and voice broadcast at the same time.

## Example
_Example: the school announces a holiday for Class 8 only. The administrator picks Class 8, sends on WhatsApp and SMS, and ten minutes later sees that 94 per cent of parents have read it._

## How each person uses it
- **Principals** oversee what goes to parents.
- **Teachers** message parents directly.
- **Administrators** send announcements and alerts.

## What you get out of it
One official channel in place of many groups, proof that a message was read, and reminders that run themselves.

## How it connects
WhatsApp Business API, Fee Management (reminders), Attendance (absence alerts), Transport (pickup and drop alerts), and AI Secretary (drafts the notices).

## Questions
- *Can we send voice messages?* Yes, voice broadcast is included.
- *Can we target one class?* Yes, class-wise or school-wide.
- *Can parents reply?* Yes, two-way messaging.

# 9. Academic Inventory Tracking

**H1:** Know what you have, where it is and when to reorder.

## The purpose
Academic Inventory Tracking keeps a live count of the school's books, uniforms, lab equipment, sports gear and supplies, and who holds them, so nothing runs out unnoticed and nothing goes missing.

## The problem it replaces
Stock is counted by hand and written in registers. Items run out before anyone notices. Nobody knows which department or person holds a piece of equipment.

## How to use it

**Set up once**
1. **Set up departments and categories** for your stock.
2. **Add each item** with a barcode or QR label.
3. **Set the low-stock level** for each item that you want to be warned about.

**Every day**
1. **Scan items in** when stock arrives and **out** when it is used.
2. **Assign equipment** to a person or department and check it back in.
3. **Act on alerts.** A low-stock alert and reorder reminder tells you before you run out.

**Every term or year**
- Review **purchase and vendor history** before you reorder.
- Use **depreciation tracking** for equipment.

## Example
_Example: the science lab's glassware drops below its set level. An alert reaches the administrator, who reorders from the vendor recorded in the history._

## How each person uses it
- **Administrators** manage stock and assets across departments.
- **School owners** review purchasing history.
- **[confirm: the team that owns stock day to day]**

## What you get out of it
A real-time stock count, alerts before shortages, a record of who has what, and a purchase history for better buying.

## How it connects
Library Management (books are stock too), Fee Management (uniform and book fees), barcode scanners.

## Questions
- *Can we scan items?* Yes, barcode and QR.
- *Do we get warned of shortages?* Yes, low-stock alerts and reorder reminders.
- *Can we track lent equipment?* Yes, check-out and check-in.

# 10. Payroll Management

**H1:** Salaries done right, every month.

## The purpose
Payroll Management turns attendance, leave and salary structures into correct salaries every month, with the statutory deductions handled, and gives each staff member a payslip.

## The problem it replaces
Salaries are calculated by hand from attendance books. PF, ESI and TDS are easy to get wrong. Payslips take days, and the bank file is typed again.

## How to use it

**Set up once**
1. **Define each salary structure.**
2. **Set the statutory settings:** PF, ESI and TDS.
3. **Record loans and advances** where staff have them.

**Every month**
1. **Bring in attendance and leave** from Staff Management and Attendance.
2. **Run the calculation.** Salaries, statutory deductions and attendance-linked deductions are calculated automatically.
3. **Review** the figures before approval.
4. **Generate payslips** and share them digitally in seconds.
5. **Download the bank-ready payment file** and send it to the bank.
6. **Send the figures to Tally** if your accountant uses it. **[confirm: whether payroll posts to Tally]**

## Example
_Example: on the 28th, the accountant runs payroll for 45 staff. Deductions follow each person's attendance. Payslips reach staff phones within seconds and the bank file is ready to upload._

## How each person uses it
- **Accountants** run payroll and send it to the bank.
- **Administrators** confirm attendance and leave.
- **Staff** receive their payslips digitally.

## What you get out of it
Accurate monthly salaries, statutory compliance, instant payslips and no retyping for the bank.

## How it connects
Staff Management, Attendance (including biometric devices), and the bank file export. Tally ERP: **[confirm]**.

## Questions
- *Are PF, ESI and TDS automatic?* Yes.
- *How do staff get payslips?* Digitally, in seconds.
- *Does attendance affect pay?* Yes, deductions follow attendance.
