## 1. Admissions (`/features/admissions`)

**Who uses it**
- **Administrators:** process admissions digitally, from enquiry to enrolment, and verify documents.
- **Principals:** approve admissions quickly.
- **School owners:** see admissions alongside fees, academics and staff on one dashboard.

**Works with**
- **Student Management:** an enrolled student's record carries on from admission.
- **Communication:** status updates and follow-ups go out by SMS and WhatsApp.
- **Fee Management:** online applications can take payment.

- **Parents:** pay fees online, with no queues.
- **School owners:** a consolidated view of fees next to admissions and academics.

**Works with**
- **Tally ERP:** fee data connects to your accounts. **[confirm: one-way or two-way]**
- **Online payment gateway:** parents pay at any time.

- **Payroll Management:** fees and salaries sit in the same finance area.
