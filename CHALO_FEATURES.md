## 1. About ChaloSchools

- **Product:** ChaloSchools (CHALO Schools Automated) — a school ERP / management platform, built by Inspace Edu Solutions Private Limited.
- **Scale:** used by 200+ schools serving 1.5+ million students.
- **Built for Indian schools:** Pre-School, State & CBSE, IB, Cambridge, Montessori and Matriculation.
- **Apps:** dedicated mobile apps for parents and students, teachers, and school management.
- **Security:** ISO 27001:2022 certified company; built around India's DPDP Act, 2023 and DPDP Rules, 2025.
- **Included with every plan:** free onboarding and data migration, free staff training, mobile apps for parents, teachers and management, data security and cloud backup.
